## Image prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cinematic website hero background for HiParcelo, an everyday useful-finds brand. Create one extraordinary editorial architectural photograph, landscape 16:9, with the calm spacious atmosphere of a luxury magazine rather than a product advertisement. Scene: viewed from inside a refined contemporary home, a tall open front doorway frames quiet rolling coastal grassland and a pale blue evening sky. Real limestone walls, a shallow warm travertine threshold and soft natural linen curtain at one edge. A single modest matte dark navy rectangular delivery parcel rests on the floor near the threshold, bottom-center, slightly to the right; real folded cardboard and paper tape, simple, entirely unbranded. The parcel must be visibly small and believable, not a giant pedestal. Empty doorway, no people. Photograph feels like arriving home to a small useful discovery. Composition: wide, serene, architectural, straight verticals, doorway centered, horizon in lower half, generous clean darker sky/stone space across the top and center for white website headline overlay later. The parcel is in the lower 25% of the frame, not behind that headline area. Natural sky-blue, warm creamy stone, muted olive grass, dark navy parcel. Lighting: late golden dusk with cool ambient sky, subtle warm sidelight and realistic soft shadows, restrained bloom, fine natural texture, gently atmospheric distance. Medium: convincingly real architectural editorial photograph, 35mm lens, natural depth of field, actual material imperfections, no synthetic CGI gloss. Do not render any text, typography, logos, UI, website mockup, borders or watermarks. Do not depict a warehouse or make documentary claims about a real company's operations. This is an illustrative brand scene.
