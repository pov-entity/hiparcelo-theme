# HiParcelo — no-spend theme development

This branch is for storefront source code, not Shopify publication.

## Operating safeguards
- No purchases, apps, subscriptions, advertising, or domain registration.
- Commerce stays disabled until a verified offer is approved.
- No invented reviews, urgency counters, medical claims, delivery promises or customer metrics.
- Product-country economics, payment eligibility and statutory policies are launch dependencies, not claims to hard-code into the theme.

## Customer journey
Homepage → first find / product landing page → specifications and usage → verified offer → cart → checkout (only after activation).
Persistent help, contact, delivery, returns, tracking and privacy links.

## Before launch
Confirm supplier sample, product documentation, first country, checkout currency, taxes/duties, delivery evidence, payment eligibility, returns address, support workflow, translations and an end-to-end test order.
